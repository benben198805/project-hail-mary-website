Featured Mission: Artemis II

The most relevant real-world mission for Project Hail Mary fans — taking the first crewed Orion spacecraft beyond the Moon.

☾ Active

Artemis II

Artemis II is the first crewed mission of NASA's Orion spacecraft, sending astronauts around the Moon and back — the closest real-world analog to the Hail Mary's interstellar journey. It tests deep-space life support, navigation, and re-entry at lunar-return velocities, paving the way for crewed Mars missions. Click to explore the full mission breakdown →

Explore Real Missions

A curated hub of active and upcoming space missions — each with a Project Hail Mary connection.

☾ Active

Artemis II

First crewed Orion flight beyond the Moon. Tests deep-space systems that mirror the Hail Mary's journey. Four astronauts, ten days, humanity's return to lunar vicinity.
⚙ Upcoming

Europa Clipper

NASA's mission to Jupiter's icy moon Europa — searching for a subsurface ocean and conditions that could harbor life. A real-world echo of Rocky's biology and astrophage ecology.
★ Active

International Space Station

The longest-running habitable outpost in low Earth orbit. A proving ground for deep-space life support, international cooperation, and microgravity science — the real-world Blip-A.
♂ Active

Mars Missions

Perseverance rover, Ingenuity helicopter, and the Mars Sample Return campaign. Searching for ancient life, testing in-situ resource utilization — a direct precursor to crewed missions.
☢ Active

James Webb Space Telescope

JWST peers deeper into the cosmos than ever before — observing exoplanet atmospheres, distant galaxies, and the signatures of potential biosignatures. The real-world Tau Ceti scanner.
☽ Upcoming

Lunar Gateway

A planned orbital outpost around the Moon supporting Artemis deep-space crewed operations. The closest real equivalent to an interstellar waystation — a stepping stone to Mars.
