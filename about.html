About PHM Explained

An unofficial deep-dive guide for curious readers of Project Hail Mary.

What This Site Is

PHM Explained is a fan-created educational commentary site dedicated to Project Hail Mary by Andy Weir. We provide detailed analysis of the book's timeline, characters, scientific concepts, and connections to real-world space exploration — all in one spoiler-aware, science-friendly place.

What We Cover

Our content is built around several core areas:

  • Timeline & Mission Breakdown — A chronological walkthrough of the Hail Mary mission from the Petrova discovery to the final choice at Tau Ceti.
  • Character Profiles — Spoiler-aware analysis of Ryland Grace, Rocky, Eva Stratt, and the supporting cast.
  • Science Explained — Accessible breakdowns of Astrophage, spin drives, Eridian biology, and the book's key scientific ideas.
  • Real Space Connections — How Project Hail Mary compares to actual NASA missions like Artemis, the Orion spacecraft, and interstellar research initiatives.
  • Book vs. Movie — Tracking confirmed and expected adaptation changes as film information becomes available.
  • Interactive Tools — Calculators, timeline explorers, and other lightweight tools for curious readers.

Who We Are

PHM Explained is created by a small team of sci-fi enthusiasts, science communicators, and web tinkerers who wanted a single, well-organized resource for everything Project Hail Mary. We are not affiliated with Andy Weir, Amazon MGM Studios, Penguin Random House, or any official franchise entity.

Our Approach

We aim to be:

  • Spoiler-aware — Clear labels and structural choices help readers avoid reveals they aren't ready for.
  • Science-grounded — We clearly distinguish between book logic, real physics, and informed speculation.
  • Legally responsible — No copyrighted full texts, no pirated materials, no unauthorized downloads. Every analysis is original commentary.
  • Reader-friendly — Clean design, fast loading, and a space-appropriate aesthetic that doesn't get in the way of the content.

Why We Built This

Project Hail Mary is a dense, rewarding book that rewards re-reading and discussion. Existing resources were scattered across forum threads, wiki entries, and video essays. We wanted a single, well-organized hub where readers could find a clear timeline, grounded science analysis, thoughtful character discussion, and real-world space context — all in one place. The site launched in 2026 and continues to grow as the film release approaches and new discussions emerge.
